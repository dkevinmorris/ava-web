About Kevin Morris

Helping Leaders Turn Strategy Into Realized Enterprise Value

Kevin Morris is the founder of Antioch Value Assurance and the author of Value Realized. His work focuses on M&A execution, enterprise value realization, technology readiness, governance, and the leadership discipline required to deliver measurable outcomes.

Built From Real-World Execution Experience

His experience spans global and domestic transactions, large-scale programs, technology workstreams, governance structures, operating model changes, TSA exits, Day-1 readiness, and benefits realization.
